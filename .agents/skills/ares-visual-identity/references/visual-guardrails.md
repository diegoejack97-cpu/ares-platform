# ARES visual guardrails

## Avoid

- purple/indigo gradients used as a shortcut for AI;
- glowing orbs, floating blobs, gratuitous glassmorphism, neon grids, and sci-fi HUD decoration;
- a sparkle icon or chatbot bubble as the universal symbol for intelligence;
- default shadcn styling left unchanged;
- `rounded-2xl shadow-lg` on every surface;
- page layouts composed only of interchangeable KPI cards;
- oversized marketing headlines inside an operational application;
- fake metrics, ornamental charts, unexplained scores, and decorative sparklines;
- generic copy such as “Elevate your workflow”, “Empower your team”, or “Unlock insights”;
- motion that delays reading or hides state changes.

## Prefer

- 12-column desktop grid and deliberate information density;
- semantic tokens with restrained surfaces and clear hierarchy;
- tabular numerals for commercial values, costs, SLAs, and timestamps;
- shape, label, icon, pattern, and text reinforcing semantic color;
- provenance, formula, freshness, attribution level, and correlation near decision-making data;
- bullet comparisons, trends, funnels, heatmaps, and relationship views only when they answer a concrete question;
- visible loading, empty, stale, partial, degraded, permission, conflict, and failure states;
- one recognizable signature behavior or visual detail derived from the ARES domain rather than fashion.
